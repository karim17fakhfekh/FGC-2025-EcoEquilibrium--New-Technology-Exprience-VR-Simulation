# FGC-2025-EcoEquilibrium-VR-Simulation
First Global Challenge 2025 VR Simulation for Team Tunisia's VR simulation subteam

# Overview and Role
I served as one of the two programmers of the New Technology Experience VR simulation. We collaborated with the research team in order to accomplish an interactive prototype for their project.
The city of Sfax is one of the largest cities in Tunisia by the land surface with olive trees planted. The olive oil sector is one of the top contributors to the Tunisian economy.
However, in recent years, persistent droughts and extreme weather conditions have severely degraded soil quality, leading to declining olive oil yields across the region.
In order to combat soil degradation, our research subteam conceptualized an automated composting unit system designed to revitalize olive groves and assist local farmers.
The Simulation included the steps of the compoting process from putting branches in the shredder until olive trees are rosed with composting liquid

# Technical Contributions
Programmed the full step-by-step composting process and system state management.
Implemented dynamic camera controls and perspective transitions to guide users through each phase.
Structured interactive scene elements, agricultural assets, and environmental logic.
Optimized rendering pipelines, lighting, and assets to maintain high frame rates.

# Global Recognition
Our project was selected in the *Top 10 Best New Technology Experience Projects* out of 192 competing nations globally at the First Global Challenge 2025.
